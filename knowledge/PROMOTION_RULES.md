# Shared Knowledge Promotion Rules

Promote a finding only when all are true:

1. It was observed directly and is supported by reproducible evidence.
2. It is non-obvious and likely to affect a future decision.
3. It is stable beyond the active task or clearly names its revalidation trigger.
4. It does not duplicate canonical child or cockpit documentation.
5. It contains no secrets, personal data, raw output dumps, or restricted source expression.
6. Project and revision scope are explicit.

Do not promote routine progress, commit history, one-off errors, unresolved guesses, task checklists, imported findings, or prose that belongs in upstream documentation. Keep candidate notes in the active `context.md`; delete them when disproved. Supersede stale findings explicitly rather than silently preserving them as current truth.
