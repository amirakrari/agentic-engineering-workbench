## Imported Skill Requirements

When importing or adapting a skill:

1. Record the canonical repository, exact path, reviewed revision, imported
   workbench paths, modifications, copyright holder, and license.
2. Preserve the exact upstream copyright, permission, and warranty notice for
   MIT material.
3. Record attribution and changes required by documentation/content licenses.
4. Treat trademarks, endorsement, privacy, patents, datasets, fonts, media, and
   separately licensed examples as independent gates.
5. Do not assume a repository-level license covers nested third-party assets.
6. Do not describe inspiration or attribution as permission.
7. Block public redistribution when no license or permission can be established.

The operational research and independent-design procedure remains in
[IP Governance](IP_GOVERNANCE.md) and the
[IP Clean-Room skill](../../.agents/skills/ip-clean-room/SKILL.md).

## Contributor and Publication Guidance

- Contributions are submitted under the repository's MIT terms only when the
  contributor has the right to provide them under those terms.
- New imported material requires a corresponding third-party notice in the same
  change.
- Source URLs and Git history alone do not satisfy notice-preservation duties.
- Removing attribution does not remove the underlying obligation.
- Before publishing a release or public mirror, resolve every entry marked
  “Not cleared for public redistribution.”
